Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

A Reel Mower Workshop ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

When you call, email or drop off a machine, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and details about your equipment and the work you want done. We only collect what we need to do the job.

How we use it

We use your details to answer your enquiry, carry out repairs, servicing, sales or hire, let you know when your machine is ready, and invoice you. We do not sell your information.

This website

This site has no forms and does not set its own tracking cookies. The map is provided by Google and the email button opens Gmail, and those services have their own privacy policies.

Storage and access

We keep your details only as long as we need them for the work, our records and the law. You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you by calling 021 0300 315 or visiting us at 28 School Road, Tai Tapu.
