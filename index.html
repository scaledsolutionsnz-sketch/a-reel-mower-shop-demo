Mower sharpening specialist

Precision sharpening, right here

Sharpening mowers is what Chris specialises in. Everything is sharpened in the workshop on precision sharpening equipment, from everyday rotary mowers to reel (cylinder) mowers and other specialised machines. A sharp blade cuts the grass clean. A blunt one tears it, and the lawn shows it.

  • Reel (cylinder) mowersThe reel and bottom blade are ground on the reel grinder, then set so they meet properly and cut like scissors.
  • Rotary mower bladesSharpened so they cut clean instead of tearing the grass.
  • Specialised mowersGot something less common? Call Chris and ask about it.

Drop it in at 28 School Road, Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.

Questions

Good to know

What mowers do you sharpen?

All kinds. Sharpening is the specialty here: reel (cylinder) mowers are ground and set on the reel grinder, rotary mower blades are sharpened, and specialised mowers can be done too. Bring it in to 28 School Road, or call Chris on 021 0300 315 if you have questions about your mower first.

What do you service and repair?

Rotary mowers, ride-on mowers and any hand-held 2-stroke gear, like chainsaws, line trimmers, blowers and hedge trimmers. Chris also does trailer repairs and more, so just ask.

When can I drop my machine off?

The workshop is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm, and closed on weekends. For a ride-on or anything big, give Chris a call first so he can plan for it.

How long will it take?

It depends on the job and whether parts need ordering in. Chris will give you an idea when you drop it off.

Can you get parts for my machine?

Trimmer line, oil and common parts are on the shelf, and anything else can be ordered in. Have the make and model handy, or the part numbers if you know them.

Do you hire out gear?

Yes, including gear like a lawn scarifier, a rotary hoe and a stump grinder. What's available changes, so call Chris on 021 0300 315 to check and book it in.
