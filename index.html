Reel mower sharpening

A proper reel grinder, right here

A reel (cylinder) mower cuts like scissors: the spinning reel shears the grass against the bottom blade. Once they're blunt they tear the grass instead of cutting it. A Reel Mower Workshop sharpens and sets them on its own reel grinder, so they cut clean again.

  1. Drop it inBring it to 28 School Road, Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.
  2. Sharpened on the grinderThe reel and bottom blade are ground on the reel grinder.
  3. Set and ready to mowThen it's set up so the reel and bottom blade meet properly.

Questions

Good to know

Do you sharpen reel (cylinder) mowers?

Yes. Reel mowers are sharpened and set on the workshop's own reel grinder. Bring it in to 28 School Road, or call Chris on 021 0300 315 if you have questions about your mower first.

What do you service and repair?

Rotary mowers, ride-on mowers and any hand-held 2-stroke gear, like chainsaws, line trimmers, blowers and hedge trimmers. Chris also does trailer repairs and more, so just ask.

When can I drop my machine off?

The workshop is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm, and closed on weekends. For a ride-on or anything big, give Chris a call first so he can plan for it.

How long will it take?

It depends on the job and whether parts need ordering in. Chris will give you an idea when you drop it off.

Can you get parts for my machine?

Trimmer line, oil and common parts are on the shelf, and anything else can be ordered in. Have the make and model handy, or the part numbers if you know them.

Do you hire out gear?

Yes, including gear like a lawn scarifier and a rotary hoe. What's available changes, so call Chris on 021 0300 315 to check and book it in.
