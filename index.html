What We Do

Specialist reel mower
services, done properly.

Whether your cylinder mower needs a full grind, your engine won't start, or you just want it tuned before the season — Chris has you covered.

⚙️
Cylinder Grinding

Precision relief grinding and bottom blade resurfacing to restore a perfect scissor action. The difference is immediately obvious after a proper grind.

🔧
Small Engine Repair

Carburettor rebuilds, ignition systems, recoil starters and fuel system repairs on all small engine mowers. Won't start? We'll sort it.

🌿
Full Mower Service

Complete strip-down, clean, adjustment, lapping and reassembly. Your mower comes back running better than it has in years.

🔩
Blade Backlapping

Fine honing of the cylinder and bottom blade together for a surgical finish. Ideal for lawn enthusiasts who want a true, stripe-ready cut.

🛠️
Push Mower Repairs

Manual push reel mowers, electric and petrol cylinder mowers — all makes and models welcome. No job too old or too obscure.

📦
Parts & Advice

Hard-to-find parts sourced, plus honest advice on whether a repair is worth it. Chris won't sell you something you don't need.
